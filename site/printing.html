3D printing — unsliced on purpose

No pre-sliced G-code here. чужой G-code crashes nozzles. We give STL (unsliced), you slice for your printer + your spool. Upload any STL on the hero 3D viewer to preview.

1 · Get the STLs

Export from Main CAD.step / Wheel Pod.step: isolate plastic body → Export STL binary (~0.1 mm chord) → 3d-printing/stl/NN-part-name.stl. Typical: TPU shell halves, pod guards, LED mount, Pi/BEC mount, battery tray, wheel locks. Ring/teeth/plates/shafts are NEVER printed.

2 · What material

UseFor
TPU 95A (Overture/SainSmart/Cheetah)shell, cradle, guards — the shock absorber. Liftoff retired HDPE/UHMW for this.
PETG / ABS-ASAjigs, pit stand, LED mounts
PLAfit-check only — shatters in fights

3 · Slice (Orca / Bambu / PrusaSlicer)

TPU 95A — direct-drive strongly preferred

Nozzle 225–240 °C, bed 40–60 °C + glue. Speed 20–35 mm/s. Retraction OFF or ≤1 mm, pressure advance OFF to start. Walls 4–6, top/bottom 5–6, infill 30–60 % gyroid (dense cradle, not hollow). Seam rear, no auto-supports in shock webs. Dry spool 65 °C 4–6 h — wet TPU = #1 failure.

PETG/ASA

4 walls, 40 %+ gyroid, brim for tall pods.

4 · Finish

Heat-set inserts M3/M4 with 200–220 °C iron — never tap TPU. Weigh prints, log in BOM. Don't print shell 100 % solid “for strength” — TPU 1.21 g/cc gets heavy; walls do the work.
